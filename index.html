Aboard in
three steps.

01
Move to steer.

Stand left to turn left, right to turn right. Everyone’s weight counts. The boat sails forward on its own.

02
Brace for the big ones.

Hold Space or the Brace button when a wave approaches. You’ll move slowly, but you’ll stay aboard.

03
Nobody left behind.

Go toward a swimming friend’s side and hold E or Rescue. Solo? Your automatic lifeline has you covered.

Collect floating supplies to repair the hull. Snacks give you a temporary edge. Reach the lighthouse together.
