A QUICK CAPTAIN’S BRIEFING

Aboard in
three steps.

01
Your crew controls the boat.

Left and right steer across an endless sea. Stand at the bow to accelerate, or the stern to slow down. The sea gets faster as time passes.

02
Dodge the moving breakers.

Big waves travel toward you. Steer around their foamy edges, or hold Space / Brace if you cross one. Islands are solid: give their shores room. Collisions jolt the whole crew upward!

03
Nobody left behind.

Overboard? Swim close to any part of the hull to climb aboard automatically. Friends can also move to your side and hold E or Rescue to throw a lifeline.

Repairs restore the hull. Twelve weird snacks bend the rules. Use one with E or the Use button, and watch its effect timer.
